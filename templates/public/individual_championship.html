Individual Scoring Rules

Individual Championship scores are calculated exclusively from points earned in individual competitions. Points won in group events are credited directly to the respective parish and do not inflate individual scores. Tie-breaking follows the official 5-level standard: Total Points → 1st Places → 2nd Places → 3rd Places → Grade A awards.
